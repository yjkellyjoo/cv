A joint project with the University of Southern California's [Autonomous Networks Research Group](https://anrg.usc.edu/www/) to improve the security of the I³ Marketplace.

The specific problem: sellers upload data products to the marketplace from IoT devices, but nothing validated those devices.
We integrated IoTcube's [hmark API](https://github.com/iotcube/API) into the marketplace to check them.

Published at the 2020 16th International Conference on Distributed Computing in Sensor Systems as *Enhancing the Reliability of IoT Data Marketplaces through Security Validation of IoT Devices* ([PDF](https://ccs.korea.ac.kr/pds/DCOSS20.pdf)).
